# Test plan — rev.0003

## Gate 0 — geometry truth map

Every modeled field must resolve to a measurement/source, a derivation, an explicit display-only assumption, or `UNVERIFIED`. A pretty mesh cannot promote epistemic state.

## Gate 1 — known-geometry fit

Reproduce Petrie exterior/chamber dimensions and Legon A–L centerline geometry. Record residuals. Do not force agreement when sources differ (for example Petrie-derived 143.8656 m vs the Egyptian Ministry 143.5 m summary).

## Gate 2 — deep-geometry existence

Before assigning a function to the controversial deep geometry, seek independent recovery of the same coordinates with methods that do not share the same inversion assumptions: microgravity, seismic methods, resistivity where appropriate, and muography where geometry permits.

## Gate 3 — topology

If boundaries replicate, test whether shafts, helices, connectors and terminals are actually continuous. Disconnected anomalies cannot support transport/hydraulic interpretations.

## Gate 4 — function discrimination

Score each hypothesis only on preregistered signatures: access/transport (traversable clearances and construction interfaces), hydraulic (connected source/head/sink and flow residues), resonant (mode localization and unusual sensitivity), magnetic/plasma (field source/topology, ionization/input, confinement, power balance, thermal path, extraction).

## Gate 5 — perturbation

Vary measured dimensions within their source uncertainty and unverified dimensions over broad priors. Compare the actual survey geometry to matched control pyramids and chamber placements. Correct for multiple tested objectives.

## Gate 6 — promotion

No hypothesis changes from HYPOTHESIS to SUPPORTED without independent evidence. Simulation output remains `SIMULATED`, never `MEASURED`.
