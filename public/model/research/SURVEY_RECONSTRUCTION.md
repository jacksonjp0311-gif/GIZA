# Survey reconstruction — rev.0003

## Coordinate contract

ARCHEON world frame is right-handed Z-up. `+X = east`, `+Y = north`, `+Z = up`. The origin is the center of the Petrie mean casing square at the pyramid base plane.

The original exterior envelope uses Petrie's adopted mean base **215.26246 m**, best casing slope **53°10′ ±4′**, and his resulting height **143.8656 ± 0.3302 m**. The Egyptian Ministry's 143.5 m original-height summary and ScIDEP's 136.4 m current-height value remain separate comparison parameters; rev.0003 does not average them.

## Passage coordinate reconstruction

The passage-axis anchor is Petrie's **490.3 in = 12.45362 m east** of the middle of the north face. Legon Table II supplies the N-S distance south from the north base and the level below/above the base for nodes A-L. This produces the major passage centerline without freehand placement.

Legon's Table I supplies segment lengths and slopes. His note 7 explicitly distinguishes extant from reconstructed entrance lengths: lower existing = **34.01 m**, upper existing = **33.88 m**. The longer AB=34.94 m and KL=36.95 m include reconstruction through lost pavement/casing. rev.0003 therefore represents each entrance as two separate ARCHEON parts: `existing` and `reconstructed`.

## Main chamber

Petrie measured the chamber at ~14.16431 m E-W × 4.97459 m N-S with wall height ~5.24256 m. Legon node I places its N-S axis at 103.23 m south of the north base and 3.55 m below the base. The X placement is derived from Petrie's corridor-axis offset and doorway offsets on the chamber's north wall.

## Lower chamber

Petrie measured the lower chamber plan at ~10.45337 m × 3.13309 m. Legon node D places its centerline level 15.34 m below base. rev.0003 does **not** claim a measured chamber height; the 2.40 m render height is explicitly display-only.
