# Cache policy

Only `media:harvest:download` may populate `cache/` automatically.

A cached record must retain the Commons source page, author/artist string, license short name, license URL when available, original URL, categories, original dimensions and a SHA-256 content hash. Files not recognized by the automated free-license filter remain metadata-only until a human reviews their rights.

Caching is not geometry promotion. Photogrammetry/metrology requires a separate FIELD registration receipt.
