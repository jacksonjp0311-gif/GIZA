# CANON-01 — Exhaustive Evidence Expansion

## Result

CANON-01 expands VAULT from a small seed registry into an evidence graph with **33 source records**, explicit source-to-object mappings for all **56 current spatial parts**, a rights/reuse matrix, conflict matrix, solver material priors, and a desktop acquisition backlog.

No geometry was promoted during this phase.

## Highest-value additions

### Open Context / ARCE Sphinx Project

The ARCE Sphinx Project 1979–1983 archive is the most important newly promoted *data source* for future expansion of the wider Khafre complex. It is published through Open Context under CC BY 4.0 and contains survey drawings, photographs, geology, notes, and stone-by-stone work around the Sphinx, Sphinx Temple, and adjacent Khafre Valley Temple.

It is not direct stone mapping of the Khafre pyramid itself. CANON therefore maps it only to future complex objects.

### AERA GPMP control network

The GPMP survey provides a high-precision site-registration precedent/control framework. It is useful for world-coordinate registration but is not treated as a downloadable stone model.

### Hölscher 1912

Hölscher provides an independent excavation-era crosscheck and broader complex measurements. Numeric facts are recorded with locators, while Heidelberg facsimile imagery remains reference-only by default because the digitization is marked In Copyright.

### Material characterization

The 2020 Heritage Science study supplies Khafre backing-limestone sample ranges useful as uncertainty-bounded priors for geomechanics and acoustics. They are explicitly not assigned uniformly to every block.

### Muography

ScIDEP is the active Khafre-specific modern muography source. Khufu ScanPyramids papers are stored only as methodological validation precedents.

## Deep claim

All 31 deep-claim model parts remain `UNVERIFIED`. The public SAR claim reporting and later retraction/methodological context have zero geometry-write authority.

## What CANON-01 intentionally did not do

- no automatic geometry mutation
- no averaging of incompatible dimensions
- no redistribution of restricted archival images
- no stone promotion from a single photograph
- no import of Khufu void geometry into Khafre
- no use of 10–30 m remote sensing as monument geometry
