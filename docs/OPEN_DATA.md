# GIZA ATLAS — Open-data/media backbone

ATLAS binds public research and reuse-friendly media to semantic 3-D objects.

## Research backbone

- W. M. Flinders Petrie, *The Pyramids and Temples of Gizeh* (1883): historical precision-survey backbone.
- Egyptian Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities: official monument context.
- Digital Giza / Harvard University: plateau archive and 3-D/site context.
- Wikimedia Commons: reusable interior/exterior photographs and reference diagrams.

## Media rule

Every image record stores the source page, author, date, license and credit. Images are remote references rather than copied into the archive, so the authoritative license record stays one click away. If the client is offline, ATLAS simply shows a no-photo state. No stock/illustrative photo is substituted for an archaeological object.
