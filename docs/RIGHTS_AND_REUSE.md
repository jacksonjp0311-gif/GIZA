# Rights and Reuse Contract

GIZA separates **scientific usefulness** from **permission to redistribute bytes**.

- Petrie: metric/text ingest allowed; preserve citation.
- Heidelberg Hölscher digitization: numeric/textual reference allowed; do not bundle facsimile images by default.
- Digital Giza: catalog/reference use; inspect record-level rights before copying any asset.
- Wikimedia Commons: check every file's individual license.
- Open Context ARCE Sphinx Project: CC BY 4.0; preserve attribution and record URIs.
- Smithsonian Eliot Elisofon images: Usage Conditions Apply; reference-only/no redistribution by default.
- NASA/USGS/Copernicus open geodata: ingest only at their native resolution with product metadata and processing receipts.
- Publisher/news sources: citation/context only unless specific reuse rights allow more.

`rights_matrix.json` is the executable policy table.
