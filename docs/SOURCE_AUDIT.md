# Source Audit — 2026-09-10

VAULT records source credibility separately from access and redistribution rights.

## High-value geometry sources

### Petrie (1883)
Historical precision-survey backbone. Original units are retained and conversions remain traceable.

### Maragioglio & Rinaldi plates via Digital Giza
Excellent plan/section cross-checks. Catalog records are public, but the package treats the plate imagery as reference-only unless explicit reuse rights are established.

### Digital Giza / Harvard
Major archive and 3-D reference environment. VAULT uses it for discovery, cross-checking, bibliography, and site relationships. It does not assume permission to redistribute Harvard 3-D assets.

## Modern non-destructive context

### ScIDEP 2025
Open-access Khafre muography project paper. The collaboration describes multi-view muography and a detailed Geant4-based simulation program. The paper states supporting data are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request; VAULT therefore does not pretend a public raw-muon dataset is bundled.

## Open visual evidence

### Wikimedia Commons
Useful exterior/interior photos and diagrams. Licenses are evaluated per file; there is no blanket image license for the whole category.

## Terrain / Earth observation

### NASA SRTM Global 1 arc-second V003
Public ~30 m elevation dataset. Appropriate for coarse plateau/regional context only.

### Sentinel-2
Free multispectral products with 10 m, 20 m, and 60 m bands. Useful for site context/change analysis; never stone-scale geometry.

### Copernicus DEM
GLO-30/GLO-90 can support terrain context, but current access conditions vary. VAULT requires an access/license check before automated ingestion.

## Rule

A source can be excellent and still be unsuitable for a specific inference because of resolution, perspective, licensing, or measurement modality.
