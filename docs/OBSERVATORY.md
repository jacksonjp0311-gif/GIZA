## Registration extension

`public/model/registration/` holds camera priors, candidate world controls, seeded jobs, survey-control sources, transform receipts and registration benchmarks. No registration artifact bypasses FIELD/VAULT promotion.

The highest-value empirical gap remains authorized modern Khafre surface data. AERA publicly lists the first laser survey of Khafre in 2025, while the Dash/AERA survey index exposes public control-network products. REGISTRATION LAB records these as acquisition priorities without inventing unavailable measurements.

## Non-negotiable rules

- ingestion is not geometry promotion;
- source discrepancies remain visible;
- sample values retain sample scope;
- public visibility is not blanket media permission;
- retracted methods have zero geometry-write authority;
- claims and evidence are separate registries;
- uncalibrated photos cannot write metric geometry;
- EXIF is not calibration;
- consumer GPS is not survey control;
- survey coordinates keep native CRS/datum until a transform receipt exists;
- residuals and uncertainty travel with every solved registration.
